# Disciples II, from Autorun's game fixes. A mouse game: the left stick moves
# the pointer, A and B are the left and right buttons (B held shows the
# encyclopedia), the right stick scrolls with the arrows. The game gives most
# keys a meaning on the map, in a city and in battle:
#   d-pad up     S  spell (map), buildings (city)
#   d-pad right  R  research, resurrect (city), retreat (battle)
#   d-pad down   D  centre on the destination (map), defend (battle)
#   d-pad left   H  heal (city)
#   ZL           A  capital (map), auto battle (battle)
#   L, R         Shift+Tab, Tab  previous, next leader; in battle the other party
#   X, Y         Enter, Esc
#   ZR           Q  quick save
#   left stick click   M      continue the move
#   right stick click  Space  end the turn, or close a window
# + and - send nothing: holding both leaves the game, and nothing is saved
# on the way. Applied over keys.txt; change any line here.
